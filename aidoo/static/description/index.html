Inside Odoo

A discreet Aidoo icon appears at the top right of every Odoo page — only for users whose email is linked to your Aidoo workspace. One click opens the panel, with three tabs: Executions, Workflows and Claude. The "Open Claude" button starts a new Claude chat that can talk to your Odoo through the connector.

A full Aidoo dashboard, in your browser

Beyond the Odoo button, Aidoo gives you a web dashboard to track requests, success rate, executions, the most-used Odoo models and your team's activity.

Save your favourite AI flows as workflows

Build reusable AI workflows visually. Each one declares its input variables (Name, dates, IDs…) that Aidoo asks the AI to fill, and the steps that read or write Odoo records.

Talk to your Odoo from Claude

Once connected, Claude can act on your Odoo: find your top customers, generate a PDF report, draft a personalised email… One sentence, your data, real actions.

Features

  • Recent executions — see what your AI assistant ran for you, prioritising the current record.
  • Run workflows — launch Aidoo workflows from Odoo with a dynamic form generated from each workflow's variables.
  • One-click Claude — open Claude.ai from the panel and start a new chat connected to your Odoo.
  • Custom-connector helper — when Claude is not yet connected, the panel shows the Name and the MCP URL with copy buttons.
  • Multi-environment — separate connection keys for Production and Pre-production.
  • Secure — connection key encrypted with Fernet (AES-128-CBC + HMAC) and bound to your Odoo database.secret.
  • Multi-language — UI ships with French, Spanish and English. Pick the language from Settings → Aidoo.

Setup

  1. Install the Aidoo module from the Apps menu.
  2. Create your Aidoo account at app.aidoo.fr (14-day free trial).
  3. On the Aidoo dashboard, go to Settings → Odoo module and generate a connection key for Production (or Pre-production).
  4. In Odoo, open Settings → Aidoo, paste the key and save.
  5. Click the Aidoo icon in the systray — you are connected.

Privacy & data

What the module sends out of your Odoo:

  • The Odoo login (email) of the current user — sent to your Aidoo workspace to identify who is calling.
  • When you open the panel on a record: the technical model name (e.g. sale.order) and the record ID — used to highlight workflows compatible with that record and to surface related executions.
  • When you launch a workflow: the variables you typed in the dynamic form.
  • No Odoo business data is read or forwarded unless an Aidoo workflow you started explicitly asks for it.

Where the data goes: only to the Aidoo backend URL you configured (default: api.aidoo.fr). The connection key is encrypted on disk with your Odoo database.secret using Fernet (AES-128-CBC + HMAC) — it never reaches the browser.

Per-company isolation: a user can only see the Aidoo panel if their Odoo email matches an Aidoo workspace member of your own organisation. No cross-workspace leak is possible — strictly enforced server-side on api.aidoo.fr.

Third parties: if you connect Aidoo to Claude (Anthropic), your prompts and any data Claude reads back from Odoo through the MCP connector are also processed by Anthropic. Aidoo's full privacy policy: aidoo.fr/en/legal.
